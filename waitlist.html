The Inheritors

He left home at 4 AM, dressed like he owned the city.

Our fathers and grandfathers were held to the jobs apartheid allowed: bakery hands, mechanics' assistants, messengers. Many still walked to work in a pressed three-piece suit. Vuwani is for the children and grandchildren who saw that, and want to carry it into today's hustle.
